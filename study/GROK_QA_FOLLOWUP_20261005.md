## Source orientation check follow-up — 16:27 UTC

The new broad bevel-fault exclusion is not a reliable concavity exception. Cross-product magnitude is twice triangle area; `row[0] > .01` skips real bevel faces up to 50 cm2. Unnormalized Z thresholds also vary with triangle size, and side faults (Z approximately zero) never fail the final `row[1] < -.002` condition. Use the local CCW edge outward vector `(dy, -dx)` instead of vertex-minus-centroid for the side/outward check; that avoids false failures at concave chipped edges. Check normalized bevel orientation, excluding only truly degenerate area. Test both triangles if the bevel quad is non-planar. No watertightness requirement is being added.

The FBX check for downward faces entirely above 1 cm is useful but does not cover lower quiet caps or side orientation. Describe that exact coverage. The post-recalculation flip above 6 mm is a repair, not proof of the original geometry. Keep the rendered review decisive; do not block the art pass on a claim of complete watertightness or replace this issue with broad test exclusions.
