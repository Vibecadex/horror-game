## What the concepts are for

The chamber is one containment room. The camera looks toward −X. The far wall is a sealed bulkhead. The near edge is a low cutaway, about 40–70 cm, around X −1540. The left wall (−Y) is pipes. The right wall (+Y) is cabinets. The centre has no roof. Teal is the key light. Two or three red practicals are pin points. One olive teddy, three darker stitchlings at 0.35 scale, one armed player in a white ground pool, a long shadow, cracked charcoal concrete.

## Beats the stills imply

These are staging notes for animation and layout. They do not add a scene.

- Idle weight sits in the body. The canonical teddy is seated in the painting. The rig is the standing sixteen-bone teddy. Do not build a new sit skeleton to match the painting. Threat, telegraph, and attack play from the existing idle.
- Telegraph lifts one arm and pitches the spine back a little, then the strike comes forward. `Teddy_Telegraph.fbx` and the shipped Attack cover that pair. `Teddy_AttackLeft.fbx` is the other arm.
- Three stitchlings stay small, darker, and low. Crawl is the shipped clip. `Teddy_SwipeLow.fbx` is the low strike. `Teddy_StitchlingIdle.fbx` and `Teddy_StitchlingFlinch.fbx` are the extra holds. There is no fourth creature.
- Defeat stays on the ground. `Teddy_DefeatBreath.fbx` breathes on the last defeat frame and does not stand.

## What concept work still does not approve

A new teddy or GLB, a Hunyuan or TRELLIS mesh, a ceiling, a truss, spotlight rows, an open hall, a second tank, crate, or barrel, a second wheel, a luminous sign, a baked pool or shadow, another flat crack card, or HUD art.
