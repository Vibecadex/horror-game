# Concept stills

5 October 2026. These are generated studies seeded from one canonical frame. They are not Unreal captures, and they do not replace [direction-close-best.jpg](../../visuals/direction-close-best.jpg).

Accepted frames stay in this folder. Frames that invent a roof, a corridor, a fourth small creature, or a painted teal puddle are in `rejected/`.

`05-floor.jpg` was checked for a cyan flood before it was filed. Cyan-pixel fraction was 0.000, and the top band was not brighter than the middle. The wide frame that followed it drew a vault-sized wheel and is in `rejected/`.

The cloth crop is the acceptance target for thread that reads as stitches. The power wall keeps cabinets on the right. Pipes stay on the left, in the shell kit, and are not taken from the rejected corridor frame.
