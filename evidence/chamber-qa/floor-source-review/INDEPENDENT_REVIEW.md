# Floor source morphology review

Inspected all four supplied Blender previews: [overview](../../../Assets/Adapted/ChamberParity/Floor/fields-overview.png), [field A](../../../Assets/Adapted/ChamberParity/Floor/field-a-detail.png), [field B](../../../Assets/Adapted/ChamberParity/Floor/field-b-detail.png) and [field C](../../../Assets/Adapted/ChamberParity/Floor/field-c-detail.png).

The connected branching, unequal plate sizes, asymmetric shard banks and absence of a strong closed outer ring are useful improvements over the existing isolated fracture islands. Advancing these sources to actual Unreal review is reasonable.

The neutral previews still make many seams read as thin drawn lines on broad, flat plates. Several long straight runs and limited secondary edge crumbling remain different from the target's more broken concrete. Source clay cannot establish final recess depth, shadowing or material integration. In the engine, check that dark troughs survive both views, raised chips have contact, bevels do not turn into bright filaments, and the coplanar outer taper does not shimmer in motion or expose a patch silhouette.

This is a **source morphology review**, not Unreal evidence, an import verification or a chamber parity acceptance. Original sources and all previews remain unchanged.
