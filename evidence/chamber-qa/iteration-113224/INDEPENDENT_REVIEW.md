# Crust V2 engine repair review

**The Crust black-cell defect is visually closed in this pair. Final chamber acceptance remains held for the remaining source audit/repair.** Both original 1920×1280 engine images and the [comparison](comparison.png) were independently inspected. [Provenance](provenance.json) preserves source hashes, complete decoding and the disclosed architectural capture method.

The cell interiors that appeared solid black in [111910](../iteration-111910/INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md) now carry visible surface value and texture. This is evident at the front near-center, both side banks and the reverse left bank. Their outlines now read as coarse cracks around intact surfaces. This engine result supports the [separate V2 orientation repair](../crust-v2-source-review/INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md); it is stronger evidence than accepting the source normal counts alone.

The reverse FOV70 and localized service fills retain the previous framing improvement: both cabinets and the far pipe section are legible. The main door, actual opposite wall and pier remain intact. No renewed material stripes, horizontal fog bar or wall-over-subject defect is apparent in these two architectural views.

The ground still reads as relatively localized coarse-crack patches over a finer-grained floor, with less varied debris/plate-edge structure than the concepts. Further material/light tuning should wait for the rest of the source audit. The integrator reports the same orientation defect in the currently active SparseSlabs source and has authorized a separate V2 repair; the three active broad fields are being checked, with repair conditional on a demonstrated defect. This review does not independently claim that all fields are faulty.
