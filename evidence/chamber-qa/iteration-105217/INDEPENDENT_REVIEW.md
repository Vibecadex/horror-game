# Chamber07 independent visual review

**The major missing architecture is repaired; chamber visual parity remains incomplete.** Inspected both original 1920×1280 Unreal PNGs at full size and the [aspect-preserved comparison](comparison.png). [Provenance](provenance.json) records original/reference hashes, complete decoding, capture receipt and held-state limitations. The concepts remain concept targets; the right column is the actual saved scene with disclosed architectural cameras, frozen characters and unchanged saved exposure.

The front bulkhead reads clearly as a layered heavy door with chamfered frame, divided leaves, a wheel, B-3 mark and red indicators. The reverse has two real separated door bays and the previously occluded central pilaster is visible. Side-wall returns, service groups and the lowered perimeter form a coherent enclosure. There is no return of the horizontal fog bar, material stripes, giant raised foundation or bright rubble filaments seen in earlier failed iterations.

The new sparse slab geometry is subdued in the delivered lighting. At full-room display size, fine mottling and thin fracture lines still dominate, with much less broad angular broken plate/debris structure than the targets. The front lower third remains substantially darker than the concept's illuminated fractured foreground, so source fragment size alone does not close this gap. Ground contact or repeated arrangement defects are not evident in these plates, but some fragment edges are too dim to assess confidently. Actual saved bounds and transform checks remain separate evidence.

The reverse's two door landmarks are present, but the outer service groups retreat into nearly black areas. Its central lit panel plane looks cleaner, paler and more uniform than the target's worn heavy construction. Red indicators remain quieter on the reverse wall. These are visible material/illumination differences; they should not be described as exact parity merely because architecture exists.
