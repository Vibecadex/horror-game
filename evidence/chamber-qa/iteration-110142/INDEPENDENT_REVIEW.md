# Patina and foreground-light review

**Foreground readability and wall ageing improve; exact chamber parity remains unaccepted.** Both original 1920×1280 plates were inspected at native size and checked against the [comparison](comparison.png) and [provenance](provenance.json).

The local foreground return reveals more of the broken floor and removes much of the previous near-floor obscurity. Patina removes the conspicuously clean wall finish. The layered front door, two actual reverse bays and central pier remain intact. No regular material-stripe, cyan fog-bar or new wall-over-body defect is apparent. The player pool and boss shadow remain distinct, though the added return softens parts of the foreground shadow.

Remaining ground differences are morphological: the actual view is dominated by fine mottling/thin crack networks and arc-like chip banks, while the targets show broader irregular broken plate edges and a richer spread of fragment sizes. Lighting has improved visibility but cannot supply the missing shape hierarchy. A bounded physical crust/debris revision is being considered by the integrator and artist; its source and actual engine result still require independent review.

Reverse side-service visibility is partly a camera issue. The [separate diagnosis](../reverse-services-diagnosis/DIAGNOSIS.md) shows that the saved cabinets and far pipe rack are at or beyond the recorded frame edges, in addition to being dark. Small practical indicators and the reverse overhead atmosphere remain quieter than the concept. A wider disclosed architectural camera and targeted environment-only accents are reasonable trials; increasing global exposure would affect unrelated successful features.

This capture is an architectural comparison with held characters. The [28-check room rerun](../runtime-105657/INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md) covers unchanged geometry before this material/light-only revision; it does not establish the current appearance or ordinary combat motion. Final chamber readback and the new native movie remain pending.
